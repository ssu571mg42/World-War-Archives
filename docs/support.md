# Support — World War Archives

## Getting help

- **Bugs or crashes:** Describe what you did and what happened (device model, iOS version). Send to the contact email listed in the App Store.
- **Feature ideas or content feedback:** We welcome suggestions. Use the same contact as above.
- **Content errors:** If you spot incorrect facts in campaigns, battles, or events, please tell us so we can correct them in a future update.

## FAQ

**The app won’t load content.**  
Make sure you have internet access on first launch if the app fetches data. Restart the app and try again.

**Map or list is empty.**  
Check the filter (battles, events, landmarks, stories) and the theater filter. Clear filters to see all content.

**Language / 语言**  
The app supports English and 简体中文. Switch the device language to change the app language.
