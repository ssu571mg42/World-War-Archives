# Privacy Policy — World War Archives

**Last updated:** 2025

World War Archives ("the App") is committed to your privacy.

## Data collection

- The App **does not** collect personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that receives your information.
- Content (campaigns, battles, events, landmarks, soldier stories) is stored locally on your device or loaded from bundled app data. No usage data is sent to us.
- The App does **not** use location services, analytics, or third-party tracking.

## Data stored on your device

- Preferences (e.g. favorites, history) are stored only on your device. We do not have access to them.
- No data is transmitted to our servers, because the App does not use any backend service.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the Support URL listed in the App Store listing for this app.
