## Description (Full)

World War Archives brings the Second World War to your pocket. Explore campaigns, battles, events, and landmarks across every major theater—from the Pacific and Eastern Front to North Africa, Western Europe, and the Atlantic.

**What’s inside**
• **Campaigns & battles** — Timeline, forces, and outcomes  
• **Events** — Key moments that shaped the war  
• **Landmarks** — Historic sites and their stories  
• **Soldier stories** — First-hand accounts and human stories  
• **Interactive map** — See where it happened; filter by theater and type  
• **Today in history** — What happened on this day  
• **Bilingual** — English and 简体中文

Browse by theater or search by name. Open locations in Apple Maps. Built for learners, history buffs, and anyone curious about the people and places of WWII.
